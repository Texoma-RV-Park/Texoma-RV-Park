Roads

  • Ritchey Trail — main 36' road, CR 167 ⇄ Hwy 82 entrance · sites 1–28, 43–62
  • Pecan Loop — 32' loop road in the east block · sites 63–81 + 3 container homes
  • Park Entrance Blvd — 70' divided entrance from Hwy 82
  • Check-In Lane — one-way loop past the office
  • Park Row — one-way 20' lane behind the amenity strip + short spur · sites 29–42
  • Office Drive — front parking aisle, exit only

Layout

  • Hwy 82 side: commercial land left open; 70' divided entrance with a 26' tree-lined green-belt median and wide curved corners at the highway.
  • Office + laundry moved onto the commercial corner beside the Hwy 82 entrance, moved south onto the front strip by the Hwy 82 entrance: 18' one-way check-in loop, 6 car stalls in front + 6 behind the office; laundry sits behind the office across Check-In Lane (exit-only onto the boulevard); RV sites now fill the old office area.
  • CR 167: second entrance/exit with wide curved corners.
  • 3 container homes (2-story, 1-bedroom) replace old site 82, inside the Check-In Lane island, facing Pecan Loop.
  • Sites 29–38 pushed back to the north line on a one-way 20' lane; in front: play area, shaded sitting area and dog park. Old dog-park corner by CR 167 is now RV sites.
  • Detention pond south of the main road at the CR 167 end: water drains north, west along a swale, down the CR 167 side through a culvert, into the pond, out to CR 167.
  • Wastewater: 3 ATUs + subsurface drip field, south of the main road next to the pond at the CR 167 end.
